A remake of the iconic Sonic the Hedgehog game! Run, Jump, and spindash through the levels!

Made with Godot.

Controls:
Arrow Keys to Move.
Up and Down to look those respective directions.
Z to Jump.
Down while running to roll.
Down Plus Z to spindash.

Touch the signs to pass the level.

Made with 0 AI! :D Don't be like Robotnik, who uses Ai!
